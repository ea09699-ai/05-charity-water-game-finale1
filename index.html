THE REASON WE FLY

Clean water changes everything.

Wings for Water uses a simple game to introduce players to a much bigger issue: access to clean and safe water. Behind every point is a chance to start a conversation about why water matters.

This independent student game is inspired by charity: water and its mission to bring clean and safe drinking water to people around the world.
